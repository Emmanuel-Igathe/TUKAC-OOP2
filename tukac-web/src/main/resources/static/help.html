🚀 Getting Started
1
Registration: New members must register with their Student ID and wait for Admin approval.
2
Profile Setup: Upload your passport photo and update your contact details in the "My Profile" section.
3
Engage: Join the "Student Forum" to start discussions or RSVP to upcoming events.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions
How do I reset my password?
Contact the Club Chairperson or Secretary. They can reset your password from the Admin Panel.
Where can I see the activity logs?
The Chairperson and Vice Chairperson can view logs under "Admin > Audit Logs" to monitor system security.
Is my data secure?
Yes, all passwords are encrypted using BCrypt, and every transaction is logged for accountability.

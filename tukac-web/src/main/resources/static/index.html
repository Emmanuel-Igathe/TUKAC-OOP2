Who We Are

A Community Built on Inclusion & Strength

The TUK Ability Club (TUKAC) was founded to create a safe, empowering space for students with physical, sensory, cognitive, and psychosocial disabilities at the Technical University of Kenya.

  • 🤝
    Peer Support Network — Connect with fellow students who understand your journey and share resources.
  • 📣
    Advocacy & Rights — We work with university management to improve accessibility and disability-inclusive policies.
  • 🎓
    Academic Empowerment — Study groups, tutoring links, and academic accommodation guidance.
  • 🏅
    Events & Competitions — Sports, cultural events, and leadership opportunities open to all members.
Our Mission Pillars

Three Pillars of Everything We Do

Every initiative, event, and decision at TUKAC is guided by these core commitments.

🛡️

Advocacy

We actively push for accessible infrastructure, inclusive policies, and fair treatment for students with disabilities across the university.

🌟

Empowerment

Through skill-building workshops, mentorship, and leadership programs, we help every member unlock their full potential.

🤲

Community

Belonging matters. We foster genuine friendships, peer support systems, and a culture where no student feels alone.
